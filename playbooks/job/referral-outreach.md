# Referral outreach playbook (job hunt)

## Who first
- Startups under 50 people: founder or the hiring manager. A founder email beats any portal.
- 50 to 500: the hiring manager or a team member in the same function, then an alum.
- Big companies (always reviewed by the person): an alum or a team member; never the CEO.

## Angles to rotate and tag (`variant.angle`)
- `their_open_role`: name the role and one line on why it fits.
- `their_recent_news`: a launch, raise or post they made, then the role.
- `shared_background`: same college, city or previous employer, only when true and sourced.
- `specific_idea`: one small, concrete observation about their product or growth that the person could help with.

Alternate angles evenly until the critic has 20 to 30 sends per angle.

## Subject styles (`variant.subject_style`)
- `role`: "ops intern role"
- `question`: "quick question"
- `name_of_thing`: "your <product> launch"

## Length (`variant.length`)
Default short (under 90 words). Medium only when the specific idea needs it.

## Lessons
- Brand inboxes (care@, support@, info@) got zero human replies; go to people.
- Follow up in the same thread on day 3 and day 7, each with something new.
