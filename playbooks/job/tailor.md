# Tailor playbook (job hunt)

## Resume
- Page count is decided per application (references/resume-formats.md): one page for finance and consulting at student level; two pages when the role draws on more relevant work than one page can hold.
- Order for students and freshers: Summary (if it earns its space), Education, Experience (or Projects before Experience when the projects match the role better), Certifications, Skills. With 3+ years of work, Experience comes before Education.
- Lead each section with the bullet that answers the JD's top requirement.
- Mirror exact JD phrases only where they truthfully describe the work.

## Cover letters
- Startups: only when the form asks. Big companies: always, and run the three writing passes (cut generic phrases; make it sound like the person; add one specific, sourced detail about them).
- Under 200 words. No "I am writing to apply for". Start with them.

## Answers
- "Why us?": one sourced fact about them plus how it links to the person's goal. Two sentences.
- Expected stipend or CTC: the profile's number; if the profile says "flexible", give the floor.
