# Researcher playbook (lead research)

Strategy the critic tunes. The process is in the agent file; this is what to search and in what order.

## Source order
1. Google Maps place pages (best for phone, website, rating, open/closed).
2. The business's own website: contact and about pages (best for email and practitioner name).
3. Directories, only to fill gaps or confirm: Practo and Lybrate for health practitioners, Justdial for local businesses, IndiaMART for suppliers. Treat directory phones as lower trust than Maps or the site (directories often show a lead-routing number).
4. Instagram bio link and Contact button, only when the profile allows Instagram and the business has no site. Read only; never message.

## Query terms by target (extend as the critic learns)
- Dietitians and nutritionists: "dietitian", "nutritionist", "clinical nutritionist", "diet clinic", "weight loss dietitian", "sports nutritionist", "PCOS dietitian", "diabetes diet consultant", "diet consultant", "nutrition consultant", "online dietitian". Small and home-based practitioners are wanted; these broader terms surface them. Exclude categories: gym, supplement store, pharmacy, ayurvedic store.
- Real-estate brokers: "real estate agent", "property dealer", "estate agency".
- Clinics: "<specialty> clinic", "<specialty> doctor".

## Collector filters (for `he collect maps --include/--exclude`)
- Dietitians and nutritionists: include `dietitian|dietician|nutrition|diet clinic|weight loss`; exclude `gym|fitness centre|supplement|pharmacy|chemist|medical store|ayurved`.

## Depth
- Per locality query, stop scrolling when two screens in a row add no new matching places.
- Website pass for every lead that has a site; it is where most emails come from.
