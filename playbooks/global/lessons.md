# Global lessons (all tracks)

Seeded from a 10-day hand-run outreach sprint (Oct 2026, ~100 sends) and the career-ops project. The critic adds to this file; each lesson says what was seen and what to do.

## Replies and follow-up
- Missed replies cost more than missing leads: a founder's DM sat unseen for 22 days and a booked call slot was missed. Every inbox and every portal inbox (Wellfound, YC Work at a Startup, Naukri, Internshala) is checked every inbox cycle.
- Reply within hours. Three of eighteen fresh applications on one portal got human replies within three days; the slow response lost one of them.
- Follow-ups go as replies in the same thread, never as a new email.
- When an interview or call is booked, message on email and the portal right away so it is on record the person showed up. Auto-scheduler invites (Calendly) sometimes fire without the founder planning to attend.

## Sources
- Job aggregators (and Indeed/Glassdoor dates) are often stale or mismatched. Prefer company careers pages and ATS boards, Wellfound, founder posts, and fresh funding news.
- Funding reports rarely give the city; check each company.
- Startup maps and directories (e.g. city startup maps listing roles with fresher-friendly flags) are strong sources for fresher roles.
- YC Work at a Startup allows 5 applications a week. Spend them on the best-fit founders first; company pages with no listed jobs still take open applications that go straight to founders.
- Wellfound lets you message only after the company opens a thread. If there is no reply, email the founder directly instead.
- Supply is rarely the bottleneck for local businesses (one Maps sweep of 10 queries gave 178 no-website businesses rated 4.3+). Message quality is.

## Contacts and addresses
- Brand care@ / support@ / info@ inboxes are customer service; 10 cold emails to them got zero human replies in 4 days. Go founder or decision-maker direct.
- Website-listed emails can bounce. Instagram's Contact button often shows an email the website lacks.
- Check that a company's domain still belongs to it before emailing: domains get re-registered by unrelated brands. Take the site from the company's YC/Crunchbase/LinkedIn page and compare the homepage title with the company name.
- Google Maps result cards hide many websites. Always confirm "no website" on the full place page before pitching a website (3 of 8 and 9 of 15 "no website" cards actually had one).

## Channels
- Phone calls beat WhatsApp for single-owner shops: asking staff "can I speak to the owner, it's about your Google reviews" got the owner's mobile. Chains pass you to head office; prefer single-owner businesses.
- Cold emails link to work (live sites, shared demos) rather than attaching. Attach a CV only for a posted role.

## Fit and legitimacy
- Check pay before investing effort: "Founder's Office Intern (Remote, Unpaid)" exists. Never pursue a listing that asks the applicant for money.
- Many Chief of Staff roles want 2+ years, but 0-year gems exist at AI startups. Non-engineering AI roles (AI ops, GEO/AEO, AI-native growth) often accept a test task instead of years.
- Established brands already run Shopify or have vendors: pitch what they lack (film, automation), not what they have.

## Measuring
- Trust a pattern only after about 20 to 30 sends of a variant. Before that, note it as a hunch.
