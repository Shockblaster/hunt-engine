# Outreach playbook (freelance)

## Channel by segment
- Funded startups and agencies: founder email; LinkedIn connect if no email.
- Local single-owner businesses: a phone call or WhatsApp by the person (prepared as a task with the sample image); email only if the owner's address is published.

## Angles to rotate and tag (`variant.angle`)
- `gap_seen`: the specific gap on their listing or site.
- `sample_first`: lead with the mock-up.
- `their_news`: funding, launch or hiring post, then the offer.
- `request_reply`: answering their public request directly.

## Pricing in the first message (`variant.price_shown`)
Test `yes` vs `no` per segment. Never below the profile's floor.

## Lessons
- Sell a small, fixed scope first ("one landing page in a week"), not an open-ended retainer.
- Do not underprice: a very low price reads as amateur to businesses with money.
