# Prospect scout playbook (freelance)

## Segments to start with (the critic reorders by reply rate)
1. **Funded early startups** (5 to 30 people, raised in the last 90 days, no in-house design or video): launch films, landing pages, decks, automations.
2. **Cash-rich local businesses with a weak web presence**: clinics, coaching institutes, interior designers, CA firms, brokers, manufacturers, premium salons and boutiques. High rating, many reviews, no or poor website.
3. **Small agencies** (white-label overflow).
4. **Explicit requests** posted in the last 72 hours.

## Buying signals (weight high to low)
Explicit request > recent funding > hiring a first marketer or designer > a launch in the next weeks > high reviews with no website > running ads with a weak landing page.

## Local sweep
Same locality method as the lead researcher. Keep businesses rated 4.3+ with 30+ reviews and a visible gap. Single-owner businesses beat chains (chains route decisions to head office).

## Daily volume
Shortlist 15, samples for the top 5, pitches for the top 10.

## Lessons
- Maps cards hide websites; confirm on the place page and by visiting.
- Brands with 10k to 20k followers usually already have vendors; prefer funded startups and cash-rich SMEs.
- Established labels already run Shopify; pitch what they lack (film, automation), not a website.
