# Choosing a resume format for one application

The tailor decides the format for every application separately. `he resume format --opp <id>` gives a starting suggestion (application system, region, paper, which file to upload, a page-count lean). These rules, plus what the company itself says, decide the rest. Record the decision and the reason in the plan's `format.why`; it is saved on the company so later applications there reuse it.

## Order of authority
1. **What the posting or the company says.** "One-page resume", "upload a Word document", a college placement template, or a careers page with resume tips (many banks and consultancies publish these). This always wins.
2. **The application system.** Workday, Taleo, iCIMS and SuccessFactors parse Word (.docx) most reliably and match exact headings (Education, Experience, Skills). Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby read a normal PDF well. ([atsverification](https://atsverification.com/blog/pdf-vs-word-for-ats/), [resumegeni](https://resumegeni.com/blog/ats-system-guide))
3. **Industry and level** (below).
4. **How much relevant material this role draws on.** Two pages only when the second page holds evidence this employer cares about, never filler.

## Page count
- **Finance (investment banking, asset management, equity research, private equity, quant, trading) for students and freshers: one page.** Recruiters treat it as a firm rule at this level. ([PrepLounge](https://www.preplounge.com/en/articles/resume-investment-banking), [eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/finance/the-absolutely-perfect-investment-banking-cv))
- **Consulting and strategy:** one page.
- **Indian campus placements and B-school drives:** one A4 page, and the placement cell's template if it has one. ([Resumevera](https://resumevera.com/blogs/campus-placement-resume), [stylingcv](https://stylingcv.com/blog/mba-resume-india-2026-mba-freshers-experienced-ke-liye-resume-kaise-banayein/))
- **Startups, product, AI and tech roles:** two pages are acceptable when the projects and shipped work are the point of the application. One page is still better if the role is narrow.
- **When unsure:** if trimming to one page would drop a line the job description directly asks for, choose two; otherwise one.

## Paper and region
- A4 for India, the UK, Europe, the Gulf and Asia-Pacific; US Letter for the US and Canada.
- No photo, date of birth, marital status or religion for India corporate, US, UK or remote roles. A photo is common but not required in parts of Europe and the Gulf; include one only if the posting asks.

## Layout rules that apply to every version
Every version is single column with real text: no tables, text boxes or images of text. Contact details go in the body, not the page header ([UIC guidance via jobshinobi](https://jobshinobi.com/blog/how-to-optimize-resume-for-lever-ats)). Use standard headings, and body text of at least 10 pt ([stylingcv](https://stylingcv.com/blog/college-placement-resume-2026-campus-interview-resume-guide-india/)). The renderer enforces all of this.

Education comes first for students. Finance recruiters read academics before experience.

A profile summary is optional. Keep it when it says something specific: the target roles, plus the one or two facts that make this person different. Drop it when one page is tight.

## Both files, every time
The renderer always writes the PDF and the DOCX. The applier uploads the DOCX for Workday, Taleo, iCIMS, SuccessFactors, or when the posting asks for Word; otherwise it uploads the PDF.

## What to record
`format.why`: one sentence, e.g. "Greenhouse, IB summer analyst in Mumbai: one A4 page, PDF upload, summary kept because it states the AM/IB target."
