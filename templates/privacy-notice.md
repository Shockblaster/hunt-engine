# Privacy notice template: lead research delivered to a client

Template only. Fill in the brackets, and have it reviewed by someone qualified before relying on it. It reflects India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules as general guidance, not legal advice.

---

**About this contact list**

[Your name / studio] ("we") prepared this list for [Client name] ("you") on [date] for the purpose of [stated purpose, e.g. inviting registered dietitians to a professional partnership programme].

**What it contains.** Each record holds only business contact details that the business or professional has published themselves: on their Google Maps listing, their own website, or a professional directory. The source of every record is listed in the file.

**What it does not contain.** No personal or residential numbers, no data from private groups or social media messages, and no data bought from third parties.

**How you may use it**
- Only for the purpose above.
- Before any telemarketing call or promotional message, check each number against the National Customer Preference Register (DND), as TRAI's rules require.
- Tell each person who you are and why you are contacting them. Stop at once if they ask, and record their opt-out.
- Do not sell or share the list, and do not combine it with other data to build profiles of individuals.
- Delete records you no longer need for the purpose, and delete the whole list on [date or condition].

**Requests from the people listed.** If anyone asks how you got their details, tell them the public source shown in the file. If they ask to be removed, remove them and tell us, so they are excluded from any future list.
